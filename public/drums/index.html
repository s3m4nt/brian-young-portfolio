Set up for the player

All left/right directions are from the drummer’s seat. Prioritize playing surfaces and cymbal centers when substituting backline hardware. Bass drum angles slightly outward to the drummer’s right; match the photos and preserve batter-head / pedal position when shell depth changes. Throne height: 21″ from the floor to the compressed seat surface, with the drummer seated. Throne placement is left to Brian.

Draft for review: near/far edge measurements establish the tilt component toward the player. Cymbal sideways tilt and exact tilt direction are not fully measured; use the photos for final alignment. Drum hoop references are retained as measured, rather than relabeled as head-center heights.
