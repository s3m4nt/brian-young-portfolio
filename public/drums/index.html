Instrument details

Heights are measured. Horizontal positions are reconstructed from approximate center-to-center distances and oriented to the photos. Stands and pedals are illustrative.

  • Backline substitutions: Prioritize playing surfaces and cymbal centers. Straight, boom, and telescoping stands can vary as long as the measured positions are reproduced.
  • Bass drum: Angle slightly outward to the drummer’s right. Match the photos and preserve batter-head / pedal position when shell depth changes.
  • Throne height: 21″ from the floor to the compressed seat surface, with the drummer seated.
  • Tilt: Near/far edge heights establish the tilt toward the player. Cymbal sideways tilt and exact tilt direction are not fully measured; use the photos for final alignment.
  • Measurement references: Drum heights are to the top of the batter-side hoop; cymbal heights are to the edge. Horizontal spacing is approximate. Stands and pedals are illustrative.
How to use this map
  1. Explore: Drag to rotate. Scroll, pinch, or use + / − to zoom. Choose Inches or Centimeters in Units; equipment sizes stay in inches. Camera buttons jump to overhead, drummer, front, or side views; Reset restores the overview.
  2. Choose an instrument: Tap its label or use the Instrument details menu. Its measurements appear on the map and in the side panel. Turn on All instruments to compare the whole kit.
  3. Place the centers: Use Overhead with Spacing enabled. Yellow lines show approximate horizontal center-to-center distances, not diagonal tape lengths between surfaces at different heights. White crosshairs mark the instrument reference centers; dashed white lines connect them to the yellow measurement plane. The kick uses the top midpoint of its batter-side hoop.
  4. Set heights and tilt: Switch to Side or 3D overview and enable Heights. Cyan lines measure vertically from the floor to near/far rims or edges. Height lines collapse in Overhead. For hi-hats, bottom and open top are separate cymbal heights; the hats are level.
  5. Reduce clutter: Work on one instrument at a time. Toggle Heights, Spacing, Cymbals, Labels, or the 6″ grid as needed. Numeric badges stay above lines and markers.
  6. Finish: Compare the reference photos, check clearances, and let Brian adjust the final playing feel. Expand the full measurement record or use Print setup reference for a paper copy.
